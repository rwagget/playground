A designer who builds

I started with a blank canvas, a head full of ideas, and no idea where any of it would lead.

What began as an obsession with making things slowly turned into a career, a collection of projects I’m incredibly proud of, and a community of people who made the journey far more meaningful than I ever expected.

Years later, I’m still learning, still making, and still incredibly grateful I decided to give this a shot.
